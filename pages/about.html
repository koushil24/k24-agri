About Us

K_24 AGRI helps Karnataka farmers look at market information before deciding what to grow.

What this project does

It brings together crop prices, market arrivals and regional information in one place, in Kannada and English.

What it does not do

It does not promise income or profit, and it does not tell anyone to change crops based on one price. Always check soil, water, season, costs and local advice.

Our data rule

We never invent prices or demand figures. Sample data is always labelled. Every real record will show its source and date.

Status: early version, built in phases

K_24 AGRI is an independent project to help Karnataka farmers explore crop demand, prices and regional markets. It is not a government service.
