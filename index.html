How K_24 AGRI helps farmers

  1. Select your region

    Choose your district and taluk.

  2. Explore crops and market demand

    See which crops are traded and how prices have moved.

  3. Compare available market information

    Put crops side by side, with dates and sources.

  4. Make an informed farming decision

    Combine this with advice from your local agriculture office.

Market information supports decision-making. It does not guarantee profit.
